# Gallery — Ezkart template 01

Gallery is a broadly reusable, image-led catalog template for homeware, fashion, beauty, food, stationery, art, gifts, electronics, and general physical retail.

## Visual system

- Grid: disciplined two-column catalog with an optional four-column density and deliberate full-width editorial interruptions.
- Type: neutral grotesk, large display scale, compact utility labels, no decorative eyebrow stacks.
- Palette: warm paper, black, cobalt, and vermilion. Merchant imagery can replace the accents without changing the layout.
- Corners: square throughout.
- Images: ordinary square or landscape catalog photography is supported; no hover-only secondary image is required.
- Motion: limited to quick-view, menu, toast, and direct interaction feedback; the page remains complete with reduced motion.

## Demo content

All product names, prices, descriptions, address details, and generated images are fictional preview content. The applied template must replace them with the merchant's real Ezkart catalog and settings. Optional sections remain designed empty or are removed when unsupported.

## Primary conversion

Browse or filter the catalog, open an accessible product quick-view, and add a real catalog product to the Ezkart cart. Full product pages remain the accessible fallback once the builder integration is defined.
