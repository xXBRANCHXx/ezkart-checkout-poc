Payment boundary

Clear responsibility

Midtrans receives what it needs to process payment.

Product selection, delivery details, and shipping choice are completed before the secure payment handoff.

Ezkart + Biteship

Own the shopping and delivery handoff.

  • Cart products, quantities, and weights
  • Customer contact and delivery address
  • Live Biteship Test rate selection
  • Server-validated courier price
  • Paid-order fulfillment reference
→
MIDTRANS

Owns the secure Snap payment experience.

  • Merchant and order reference
  • Final amount to be paid
  • Item and shipping details
  • Customer contact for the transaction
  • Notification and return destination
✓
Sensitive credentials remain with the payment provider.

Ezkart never requests or stores the customer’s card number, PIN, OTP, banking password, or wallet credentials. Midtrans sends a signed notification so Ezkart can update the order.
