## Owner corrections incorporated

- Ezkart uses DOKU's direct API and its own checkout. The platform fee means
  **commission**. FlexiBill is DOKU's recurring billing protocol.
- Every seller's Ezkart Wallet is backed by one DOKU Sub-Account; withdrawals use
  the onboarding bank. Existing uncertain enrollments and started withdrawals
  retain their original references and destinations.
- Ezkart's commission-to-company-bank workflow is distinct from seller payout.
  DOKU's [Collect and Route guide](https://docs.doku.com/wallet-as-a-service/sub-account/collect-and-route)
  distinguishes routed Sub-Account funds from ordinary automatic main-bank
  settlement. A registered bank does not prove a configured Sub-Account sweep.
- The government declaration concerned **storing age**, not promising full ID
  verification. The premature empty identity gate was not deployed. The checked
  distinction and separate accuracy obligations are recorded in
  [seller age declaration](seller-age-declaration.md).
- An OpenRouter key named Ezkart Jev Beta was created with a USD 5 total cap and
  27 December 2026 expiry, stored privately, and left unused/unconfigured. No
  model call or automatic enforcement is enabled. The requested page archive,
  re-scan and termination lifecycle is recorded in [Jev page review](jev-page-review.md).
