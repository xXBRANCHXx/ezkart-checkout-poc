# Payment brand assets

`bca.png` is the BCA logo supplied by DOKU's payment UI for
`VIRTUAL_ACCOUNT_BCA`, downloaded unchanged on 2026-09-19:

https://cdn-doku.oss-ap-southeast-5.aliyuncs.com/doku-ui-framework/doku/img/bank/bca.png

This source is referenced by DOKU's own hosted checkout asset mapping. The logo
identifies the bank for the BCA Virtual Account method; it is not an Ezkart
security certification or endorsement. BCA and its logo are trademarks of
PT Bank Central Asia Tbk. Preserve its colors and aspect ratio.

BCA's brand reference:
https://www.bca.co.id/id/tentang-bca/media-riset/pressroom/Brand-Assets

Payment typography uses the self-hosted, unmodified Inter variable font from
https://github.com/rsms/inter (file `docs/font-files/InterVariable.woff2`).
Its SIL Open Font License is included alongside the font in
`cart/admin/assets/fonts/inter-OFL.txt`.
